# PhoenX evergreen marketing queue

This folder is the source for a future, evidence-led evergreen marketing system. It is not connected to a publisher and does not post anything.

## Rules

- Every factual claim must point to a source in the catalogue, an original release page, an approved document, or another recorded evidence location.
- Keep unknown dates, credits, recording stories, and rights status unknown. Do not manufacture a personal story to fill a calendar slot.
- Use only assets that are owned or cleared for the proposed use. Lyrics, photographs, artwork, and third-party footage need a rights check before publication.
- Every draft needs human approval before it can be scheduled or published.
- Do not post the same call to action repeatedly. Record last-used date and a cooldown.
- Do not publish daily just to satisfy a schedule. A smaller amount of substantiated material is better than spam.

## Queue fields

`id`, `theme`, `catalogue_id_or_slug`, `draft_brief`, `evidence_source`, `rights_status`, `target_url`, `channel`, `approval_status`, `last_used`, `cooldown_until`, `notes`.

## Starter rotation (planning only)

1. A release spotlight based only on verified title/year/type.
2. A chronological catalogue guide linking to permanent release pages.
3. A “start here” listening guide once track recommendations are approved.
4. A video spotlight after the video is verified as official and correctly matched.
5. An artwork or photograph feature after provenance and rights are checked.
6. A documented recording/production story only when a source exists.
7. A Greek/English catalogue navigation post based on verified language metadata.

These are editorial briefs, not approved posts. Do not publish them until source and rights fields are completed.
